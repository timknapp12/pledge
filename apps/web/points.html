How Points Work

Pledge rewards you with points for being active and achieving your goals. Points are tracked per season and accumulate to your total lifetime score.

Earning Points

1

Create Pledges

Earn 10 base points for every pledge you create, plus 10 points per $1 USDC pledged. Duration bonus (50 points per week) is awarded when the pledge is completed, based on actual time elapsed.

2

Complete Goals

At completion, your creation points plus duration bonus are multiplied based on completion:

  • 100% completion: 2x multiplier
  • 50–99%: 1x multiplier
  • 1–49%: 0.5x multiplier
3

Build Streaks

Consecutive 100% completions multiply all points earned:

  • 2 in a row: 1.25x multiplier
  • 5 in a row: 1.5x multiplier
  • 10 in a row: 2x multiplier

Referral Program

Every Pledge user gets a unique referral code. Share it with friends and you both benefit:

Refer Friends

  • 25 bonus points for you when a friend signs up with your code
  • 25 bonus points for your friend too
  • You earn 10% of their pledge points for the rest of the season

Your referral code is in the app under Profile → Points & Referrals.

Seasons

Points are organized into seasons. Each season may have different earning rules and multipliers. Your total lifetime points are the sum of all seasons.

Season 0 is currently active with the rules described above. Future seasons may introduce new ways to earn points, leaderboards, and rewards.

Example

You create a 2-week pledge pledging $20 USDC:

  • Base creation: 10 pts
  • Pledge bonus ($20): 200 pts
  • Creation total: 210 pts

You complete 100% after the full 2 weeks:

  • Duration bonus (2 weeks): 100 pts
  • Subtotal (creation + duration): 310 pts
  • 100% completion → 2x multiplier = 620 pts

If this is your 5th consecutive 100% completion, the streak multiplier adds another 1.5x = 930 pts.

Note: If you close a pledge early, duration points are based on actual time elapsed — not the original deadline.
